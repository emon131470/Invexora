PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your information

A plain-language explanation of what we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 2026

This policy applies to the INVEXORA website. INVEXORA is a demo / simulation platform: balances, profits and commissions are virtual and no real money is accepted or paid out. We still treat the personal information you give us with care.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your first and last name, email address, phone number and the referral code you used (if any).
  • Activity on the platform: plans you choose, deposit and withdrawal requests (amount, payment method and note), daily profit records and referral records.
  • Support messages: the messages you send through the support chat and the replies you receive.
  • Technical data: a login session stored in your browser so you stay signed in, and basic server logs such as time of request.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and verify your email with a one-time code.
  • To run the platform: show your balance and history, review requests and calculate referral commission.
  • To answer your support messages and fix problems.
  • To protect the site from abuse, such as automated sign-ups or repeated failed attempts.

3. Who can see your information

You can only see your own data. Platform administrators can see account, request and support information so they can review requests and help you. Referral partners only see a masked email address (for example ab***@gmail.com) and the name you registered with. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the site: a database and authentication provider (Supabase) to store your data, an email service to send login codes, and a web host (GitHub Pages) to serve the pages. These providers process data only to deliver their service.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to make the site work. We do not use advertising cookies. If a security check (CAPTCHA) is enabled, its provider may set the cookies it needs to tell humans from bots.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account and activity records while your account exists. Support messages are kept so we can follow up on earlier problems. You can ask us to delete your account and related data at any time.

7. Your choices

  • Ask to see the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Stop using the site at any time.

To make a request, send us a message through the support chat or the Contact page.

8. Security

Data is stored on the server with access rules so each user only reads their own records. Sign-in uses one-time email codes instead of passwords. No online service can promise perfect security, so please keep your email account safe and never share your login code.

9. Children

This site is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Please check it from time to time.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the Contact page or the support chat.
