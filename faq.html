FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about accounts, plans, deposits, withdrawals and referrals.

What is INVEXORA?

INVEXORA is a genuine investment platform. You can register, choose a plan, submit a deposit, track daily profit, refer friends and request withdrawals, all with virtual balances.

How do I create an account?

Click Register, enter your name, email and phone, then type the one-time code sent to your email. Your account is ready right after verification.

How does login work?

Enter your email on the Login page. We send a one-time code to that address. Enter the code to sign in. There is no password to remember.

I did not receive my code. What should I do?

Check your spam folder, wait a minute and use Resend code. Make sure you typed the email correctly. Requesting too many codes in a short time can temporarily block new ones.

How do deposits work?

Choose a plan, enter an amount inside its range, pick a payment method and submit. An administrator reviews the request. When it is approved, your plan starts.

When is daily profit added?

After approval, the plan's daily rate is added to your balance once per day until the plan ends. You can follow each day in My Investments.

How do I withdraw?

Open the Withdraw tab in your dashboard, enter an amount up to your available balance (above the minimum) and submit. An administrator reviews the request.

How does the referral program work?

Share your code or link. When someone registers with it and their deposit is approved, a commission for that plan is added to your balance.

Can I change the plan after I start?

A running plan keeps its original terms. You can start another plan by submitting a new deposit.

Where can I get help?

Use the Contact page and we will reply by email.
